# FrameFit Privacy Policy

**Publisher:** Gabriel Polak  
**Contact:** [gabriel.polak.1986@gmail.com](mailto:gabriel.polak.1986@gmail.com)  
**Application:** FrameFit (`sk.framefit.app`), version 1.0.0  
**Effective date:** 24 October 2026

## 1. Local photo processing

FrameFit processes selected photos, camera captures, frame images, pattern samples and project settings locally on your phone. The app has no account, advertising, analytics or internet permission. We do not receive or upload your photos through the app.

## 2. Camera and selected images

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo, photograph a frame or capture a pattern.

Android's system photo picker grants access to images you select without giving FrameFit access to your entire photo library. Android or your selected photo provider may use its own cloud services.

The app does not request advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location or microphone access.

## 3. Information stored on your device

FrameFit keeps one work-in-progress project in its private app storage. It may contain:

- A private original photo and a working copy for editing and high-quality export.
- An optional photograph of a frame.
- A selected 1024 × 1024 pattern sample.
- Project settings and editing adjustments.

EXIF orientation is applied when processing an image. The privately retained original may contain its original metadata. Original metadata, including location tags if present, is not copied into rendered photo exports.

## 4. Saving, sharing and printing

Saving, sharing or printing happens only on your instruction. You choose the storage provider, receiving app or print service. Their own privacy terms apply, and a provider you select may use the internet.

We do not control copies retained by recipients or other services you choose.

## 5. Retention and deletion

Deleting the project removes its private original, working photo, frame image, pattern sample and project settings.

Images already saved to the gallery or files, and copies sent to other apps or recipients, remain until you remove them separately.

Temporary export files older than 24 hours are removed **at the next export**. They are not necessarily deleted immediately when they reach 24 hours. Android may clear the app cache sooner, and you can also clear it in Android settings.

Uninstalling FrameFit removes its app-private data. App backups are disabled.

## 6. Intended audience

FrameFit is intended for people aged 13 and older. The app does not include an age-verification system.

## 7. Support and privacy requests

If you contact the publisher by email, the information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request.

For privacy questions or requests, contact [gabriel.polak.1986@gmail.com](mailto:gabriel.polak.1986@gmail.com).

## 8. Frame recognition

Frame detection uses experimental edge and geometry processing on your device. Version 1.0.0 uses the CPU; NPU acceleration is not bundled. Confirm the frame's real dimensions before printing.
