FrameFit — Privacy policy

Publisher: Gabriel Polak
Contact: gabriel.polak.1986@gmail.com
Effective date: 24 October 2026

FrameFit processes selected photos, camera captures, frame images, pattern samples and project settings locally on your phone. The app has no account, ads, analytics or INTERNET permission. We do not receive or upload your photos.

Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker grants access to selected images, without access to your whole library. Android or your selected photo provider may use its own cloud services.

A private original and working copy are stored for editing and high-quality export. An optional frame image, a 1024 × 1024 pattern sample and one project are also stored privately. Original EXIF orientation is applied; original metadata, including location tags if present, is not copied into rendered exports.

Saving, sharing or printing happens only on your instruction. You choose the storage provider, receiving app or print service, whose own privacy terms apply. A provider you select may use the internet. We do not control recipient copies.

Deleting the project removes its private original, working photo, frame, pattern and project settings. Saved gallery/files and recipient copies remain until you remove them. Temporary export files older than 24 hours are removed at the next export; Android may clear app cache sooner. Cache can also be cleared in Android settings. Uninstalling removes app-private data. Backups are disabled.

The intended audience is age 13 and older. There is no age-verification system. No advertising identifiers, contacts, precise location or microphone access are requested.

If you contact the publisher by email, information you voluntarily provide is used to respond to your request. For privacy questions or requests, use the contact above.

Frame detection is experimental edge/geometry processing. This release uses CPU; NPU acceleration is not bundled. Confirm real dimensions before printing.
